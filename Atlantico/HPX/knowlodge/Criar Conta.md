Documentation for Creating an Account on SmartHP and PIE/STAGE 

Creating an Account on SmartHP 

Creating an Account via the SmartHP App 

1. Open your browser and search for YOPMAIL, it is usually the first link. Check at [https://yopmail.com](https://vscode-file//vscode-app/c:/Users/SoAn843/AppData/Local/Programs/Microsoft%20VS%20Code/resources/app/out/vs/code/electron-sandbox/workbench/workbench.html). 
    

2. On the right side, below the ads, there is a field named "Type the Email name of your choice". 
    

3. Enter only the desired email name and select the arrow to the right of this field. 
    

4. At the top, just below the YOPMAIL logo, you will see your email. Copy it. 
    

5. Open the SmartHP app. 
    

6. Select "Create account". 
    

7. An HP page to create the account will open. Enter your first and last name. In the email field, paste the email created in YOPMAIL. 
    

8. Enter an easy-to-remember password or write it down so you don't forget, e.g., Teste123@. 
    

9. Select "Create". 
    

10. Go back to the YOPMAIL page, copy the code sent to your email, and paste it on the HP page. Select "Verify". 
    

11. Select "Open SmartHP". 
    

12. Done, account created. 
    

Creating an Account on PIE or STAGE via Link 

Links: 

- PIE: [https://consumer.pie.portalshell.int.hp.com/](https://vscode-file//vscode-app/c:/Users/SoAn843/AppData/Local/Programs/Microsoft%20VS%20Code/resources/app/out/vs/code/electron-sandbox/workbench/workbench.html) 
    

- STAGE: [https://consumer.stage.portalshell.int.hp.com/](https://vscode-file//vscode-app/c:/Users/SoAn843/AppData/Local/Programs/Microsoft%20VS%20Code/resources/app/out/vs/code/electron-sandbox/workbench/workbench.html) 
    

Steps: 

1. Select the link where you want to create the account, either PIE or STAGE. 
    

2. Open your browser and search for YOPMAIL, it is usually the first link. Check at [https://yopmail.com](https://vscode-file//vscode-app/c:/Users/SoAn843/AppData/Local/Programs/Microsoft%20VS%20Code/resources/app/out/vs/code/electron-sandbox/workbench/workbench.html). 
    

3. On the right side, below the ads, there is a field named "Type the Email name of your choice". 
    

4. Enter only the desired email name and select the arrow to the right of this field. 
    

5. At the top, just below the YOPMAIL logo, you will see your email. 
    

6. Open the PIE or STAGE link. 
    

7. Select "Create account". 
    

8. Enter your first and last name. 
    

9. Go back to YOPMAIL, copy your email, and paste it on the account creation page. 
    

10. Enter an easy-to-remember password or write it down so you don't forget, e.g., Teste123@. 
    

11. Select "Create". 
    

12. Select "Continue". It is not necessary to enter a phone number. 
    

13. Select "Continue". 
    

14. Done, account created. 
    

Add devicec HPSA
