### JOB INTERVIEW

Here are some common interview questions for a software engineer position:
1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why do you want to work here?
3. What’s your greatest strength and weakness as a programmer?
4. Tell me about a difficult bug you had to fix. How did you solve it?
5. Where do you see yourself in five years?
