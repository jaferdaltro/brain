Congratulations on your participation in our class "Cash or Credit" today! We talked about an idiom or expression that isn't literal. "to take out/to pay back" That doesn't mean that you put money on someone's back. It just means that you withdraw money from the bank, or you give money back to someone because they lent you money first. Ex. My brother lent me $20, so I have to pay him back tomorrow. Keep listening for idioms!
