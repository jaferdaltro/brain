Perhaps what he just articulated (ineloquently) in this very short clip, is that X (formerly Twitter) was first and foremost a channel for information. Free from advertising sway. It was an unencumbered news channel. Particularly for populations that didn’t have access to information through regular media channels or that had governments controlling what information they were exposed to. Maybe not great business or brand practice, but what he’s saying is that the platform SHOULDN’T be funded by mega corporations. Maybe freedom of speech and difference of opinion CAN be a little more free. (Hate speech and fire drills excluded.) You may not agree with Elon Musk’s personal politics. But if X/Twitter was devoid of advertising dollars and instead continued to be a marketplace for ideas and expression, it seems that it would serve the masses more than it would corporate agendas. Critical thought and disagreements should occupy the same spaces. It’s not until we understand each other that we achieve a higher level of consciousness.
