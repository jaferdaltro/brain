In engineering, EVT stands for Engineering Validation Test, 
DVT stands for Design Validation Test, and 
PVT stands for Production Validation Test. 

**Engineering Validation Test (EVT)** is the **first phase** of testing in the product development process, where **prototypes** are tested to verify that they meet **design specifications** and **requirements**. 

**Design Validation Test (DVT)** is the phase where the **design is tested** to ensure that it meets the intended requirements and that it can be manufactured at scale. 

**Production Validation Test (PVT)** is the **final phase** of testing before mass production begins, where the production process and equipment are validated to ensure that the product can be **consistently manufactured** according to specifications.
