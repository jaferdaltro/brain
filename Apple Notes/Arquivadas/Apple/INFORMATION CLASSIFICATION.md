### STRIDE

- SPOOFING
	- When a person or program successfully identifies as another by falsifying data
- TAMPERING
	- Intentional modification of data or system to make it **harmful** to others.
- REPUDIATION
	- False denial of an action or transaction with no way to prove otherwise.
- INFORMATION DISCLOSURE
	- Unwanted dissemination of data, tecnology, or privacy.
- DENIAL OF SERVICE 
	- Making data or a machine or network resource unavailable for intended users.
- ELEVATION OF PRIVILEGE
	- Gaining unauthorized access to resouces of features that are normally protected.
